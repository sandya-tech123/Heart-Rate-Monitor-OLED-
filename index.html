OLED Heart Rate Monitor

Simulator of a MAX30102 pulse sensor driving a 128×64 SSD1306 display. Change the pulse, add noise or lift the finger and watch the detector respond, the same way the Arduino firmware does.

Detection: 50 Hz samples, 3-point smoothing, adaptive threshold, 300 ms refractory period, readings outside 30–220 BPM rejected, last 4 beats averaged. The firmware is in firmware/heart_rate_monitor/ and the full write-up is in docs/PROJECT_REPORT.md in the project repository. Not a medical device.
